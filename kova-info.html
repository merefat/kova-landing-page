Kova Platform & Feature Architecture

Kova is a local-first smart home platform designed by ClickBIT. Running directly on a local in-home hub, it talks directly to hardware without requiring cloud connectivity. Kova organizes home automation around time rhythms and house state (modes) rather than unorganized device lists.

Modes & Rhythms

The house is always in a Mode (Day, Evening, Wind down, Night, Dawn). Each mode sets device targets, triggered by a Rhythm:

  • Clock time (e.g. 07:00)
  • Solar events (Dawn, Dusk, Sunset ± offset)
  • Islamic prayer times (Fajr, Maghrib ± offset)
Overlays & Moments

Overlays are temporary overrides (Movie, Away, Party, Guest) ending by time, device-off, arrival, or manually.

Moments are one-off timed daily actions (e.g., "21:00 rain sounds").

Full Rules Engine

WHEN: State transitions, sensor thresholds, device events, time/solar/prayer, recurring intervals, presence events, hub boot.

ONLY IF: State, reading, overnight windows, days, who's home, active mode, room activity, nested ALL/ANY/NOT.

THEN: Set devices, delays, wait-until, notifications, overlays, if/else, repeat, announcements, value ramps, Sage tasks.

Explainability & Undo
  • Event Log: Every change logs its cause (mode, moment, overlay, behaviour, user, device, presence, assistant, automation, undo, system).
  • "Why?" Feature: Explains instant reason behind device states.
  • 15-Minute Undo: Any user action can be rolled back.
  • Day Preview: Visualize today's full plan at any hour.

Sage Assistant ("Ask Kova")

Multi-Tier AI & Local Parser
  • Local Deterministic Parser: Zero cost, instant local intent execution.
  • Learned Phrases: Replays previous clean AI responses locally.
  • Supported LLM Engines: Anthropic (prompt caching), MiniMax, OpenAI, OpenAI-compatible servers, or local LLMs.
  • Two Tiers: Everyday (Claude Haiku 5-5) + Main reasoning (Claude Sonnet 5-5), routed by heuristics or ClickBIT's Jev router.
  • Hard Guardrails: Max 12 rounds/request, default $8/month budget. Background work halts at 60% daily share; owner notified at 80%.
Hub-Enforced Rules (Bypassing LLMs)
Non-Negotiable Hub Invariants
The LLM cannot hallucinate or overstep:
  • Change ONLY what was asked — unsolicited full-house rewrites are blocked.
  • Detail edits rather than destructive rebuilds.
  • No claims without an actual successful tool execution result.
  • User "No" changes nothing; errors and failures are clearly communicated.
  • One-tap confirmation required for deletions, permission grants, or high autonomy actions.

Presence & Notifications

Multi-Signal Presence ("Who's Home")

Combines 5 independent inputs per person to ensure accurate presence:

  • OPNsense Router: ARP and NDP table sweeps every 30 seconds.
  • Warden Router: Device tables and resident mapping.
  • Network Ping: TCP 62078 probe designed for sleeping iPhones.
  • Phone Automations: Shortcuts / Android POST with per-person key.
  • Native App Geofence: 150m boundary background monitoring.
Conflict Policy: Arrival wins instantly. Phone "left" beats router for 15 minutes. 10-minute away debounce prevents transient drops.
Alerts & Notifications

Channels: Web Push (VAPID), Expo Push (APNs/FCM), and ntfy (zero-setup HTTP).

  • Doorbell ring with snapshot attachment.
  • "Everyone's out with lights on" with a 1-tap Turn them off action.
  • Device offline (10m) & Link loss/restoration (5m).
  • Warden security events (WAN down/failover, rogue device, blocked threats).
  • Proactive snoozable insights: filter life, air quality, open doors (>N min), low battery, weather warnings.
  • Quiet hours and custom rate limits (every time, 1h, 6h, daily, never).

Security Devices & Safe Easing

Asymmetric Security Safety Standard

Security devices (Locks, Garage Doors, Alarms) are never treated as trivial on/off switches.

Securing (Lock / Close / Arm):

Runs freely across automations, modes, voice bridges, Sage, and apps.

Easing (Unlock / Open / Disarm):

ALWAYS requires an explicit one-tap grant via memory-only SecureAsk cards (10 min TTL). Enforced at Registry.command.

  • Read-back Verification: 20–30s verification cycle confirms physical latching; jammed latches trigger instant alerts.
  • Zero Voice Exposure: Easing commands are strictly excluded from Apple Home, Google, and Alexa bridges.
  • Biometric Protection: Face ID / Touch ID enforced in native app before easing.

Rooms, Climate & Smart Zoning

Ducted AC Zone Management

Physical motorized dampers (open/closed and percentage opening) mapped directly to rooms.

  • Exposed to Apple Home, Google Home, and Alexa as independent virtual thermostats.
  • "Turn on the AC in Bedroom" opens that zone damper, detects room temperature, considers season and weather forecast, and targets comfort baselines (Cool 24°C / Heat 21°C).
  • Conflict arbitration prevents one room from switching the compressor to heating while other zones are cooling.
Spatial Organization
  • Rooms with custom iconography and ordering.
  • Room Groups (e.g., "Upstairs", "Downstairs").
  • Interactive Floor Plan Canvas: Draw rooms and walls, upload blueprints, place devices, and align robot vacuum maps.
  • One-tap "All Off" per room.
